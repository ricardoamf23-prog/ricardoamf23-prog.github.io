Privacy policy

This is a personal, single-user application. It uses the Google Drive API (through rclone) only to sync the owner's own notes between their computer and their own Google Drive.

The app does not collect, store, share, sell or transmit any user data to third parties. Files and access tokens stay on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
